**Overview**
PokéAdventure VR transports you into a living, breathing Pokémon world entirely in virtual reality. As a trainer, you’ll physically reach out to grab and throw Poké Balls, teleport between lush environments or smoothly walk with your joystick, and uncover hidden secrets by interacting with objects and portals. Intuitive UI panels and hover-activated info displays keep you informed without pulling you out of the experience, while cinematic cutscenes and haptic feedback heighten immersion. Built with Unity’s XR Interaction Toolkit and optimized for Oculus Quest, every interaction is designed to feel natural, engaging, and uniquely Pokémon.

**Various Interactions**

* **Grab & Release** – Pick up Poké Balls, berries, and environmental props with physics-based realism.
* **Throw & Catch** – Aim and launch Poké Balls to capture roaming Pokémon, complete with trajectory preview.
* **Teleportation & Smooth Locomotion** – Instantly jump to distant areas or smoothly walk using your controller’s joystick.
* **Hover Highlights** – Point at objects or Pokémon to reveal outlines and pop-up info panels.
* **UI Menu Navigation** – Open, scroll, and select menu options on an in-world canvas using direct touch or pointer rays.
* **Portal Activation** – Step through glowing gateways to switch scenes (e.g., grocery store, trivia room).
* **Cutscene Triggers** – Walk into designated zones to launch full-screen videos that temporarily disable input.
* **Environmental Triggers** – Activate sound effects, particle bursts, or background music when entering special areas.
* **Haptic Feedback** – Feel controller vibrations on successful throws, captures, or collisions.
* **Gaze Selection** – Look steadily at distant items to select and interact without a physical grab.
* **Inventory Management** – Open and organize your Poké Ball and item list through an interactive wrist-mounted panel.
* **Audio Cues & Voice Prompts** – Hear Pokémon cries, ambient sounds, and contextual guidance as you explore.
